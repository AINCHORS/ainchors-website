My practice is focused exclusively in representing individuals and small to mid size businesses in resolving debt issues, protecting assets from creditors, and litigation involving the same. My background is in creditor side litigation, representing large financial institutions in foreclosure, eviction, and bankruptcy matters.

I currently live in beautiful Mill Creek, WA with my wife Nellie, our son Isaac, and our dog Remy.

During my free time I enjoy organic cooking, travelling, martial arts, reading, and rooting for the Seahawks, Mariners, and Sounders.
